Privacy Policy

At EstateHaven, we're committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have over it.

Last Updated: 1 October 2025

We never sell your personal data

EstateHaven is fully compliant with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India. Your data is used strictly to operate the Platform and connect you with genuine buyers or sellers.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy describes how EstateHaven ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you use our website, mobile app, and services. By using EstateHaven, you consent to the practices described in this policy.

1 Introduction

EstateHaven is India's zero-brokerage real estate platform that connects property owners directly with buyers and tenants. To provide this service, we need to collect some information from you.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users — property owners, buyers, tenants, and visitors — and covers all data we collect through our website, mobile app, and related services.

We comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and other applicable privacy laws. If you have any concerns, please contact us.

2 Data We Collect

We collect different types of information depending on how you use EstateHaven:

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

Category Examples
Identity Full name, gender, date of birth
Contact Mobile number, WhatsApp number, email address, alternate phone
Address Home address, city, state, PIN code
ID Proof Aadhaar number, PAN, Passport, Driving License, Voter ID (for verification only)
Property Details Property type, BHK, area, location, photos, price, amenities, description
Account Password (encrypted), preferred language, referral source

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, device model
  • Log Data: IP address, pages visited, time and date of visits, time spent on page
  • Location Data: Approximate location based on IP address (not GPS)
  • Usage Data: Properties viewed, searches performed, filters applied, favorites saved
Important: Your ID proof number (Aadhaar/PAN/etc.) is used only for one-time verification and is never shown publicly or shared with other users. Only your name, WhatsApp number, and email are visible to buyers.

3 How We Collect Your Data

We collect information through:

  • Direct interactions: When you register, post a property, contact us, or update your profile
  • Automated technologies: Cookies, web beacons, and similar tools that track usage
  • Third parties: When you login via social media or share content from our platform
  • Communication: Emails, WhatsApp messages, support tickets, and phone calls

4 How We Use Your Data

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

4.1 To Provide Our Services

  • Create and manage your account
  • Publish your property listings on the Platform
  • Display your contact details to interested buyers/tenants
  • Enable direct communication between owners and buyers

4.2 To Improve Our Platform

  • Analyze usage patterns to enhance user experience
  • Fix bugs, resolve technical issues, and optimize performance
  • Develop new features based on user feedback

4.3 To Communicate With You

  • Send account-related notifications (verification, password reset)
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Send promotional emails or newsletters (only with your consent — you can opt out anytime)
  • Notify you of important policy updates

4.4 To Ensure Safety & Security

  • Verify your identity during registration
  • Detect and prevent fraud, spam, or abuse
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and zero-broker policy
  • Comply with legal obligations

5 Data Sharing & Disclosure

EstateHaven does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for commercial purposes. We only share your data in the following limited circumstances:

5.1 With Other Users (By Design)

When you post a property listing, the following information is visible to all visitors of the Platform:

  • Your name
  • Your WhatsApp number
  • Your email address (optional)
  • Property details and photos

This visibility is essential to enable direct contact between owners and buyers.

5.2 With Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate the Platform:

  • Cloud hosting: For secure data storage
  • Email/SMS services: To send OTPs and notifications
  • Analytics tools: To understand usage patterns (anonymized data only)

All service providers are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by:

  • Indian law, court order, or government request
  • Law enforcement agencies investigating fraud or illegal activity
  • Legal proceedings related to your account
Note: We never share your ID proof, password, or payment information with anyone — including other users.

6 Cookies & Similar Technologies

EstateHaven uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience. Cookies are small text files stored on your device.

6.1 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the Platform to function (login, session management)
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our site
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and choices (language, city)
  • Marketing Cookies: Show relevant ads on other platforms (only with consent)

6.2 Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of EstateHaven. For more details, see our Cookie Policy.

7 Data Security

We take the security of your personal data very seriously. Here's how we protect it:

7.1 Technical Measures

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Password Hashing: Your password is hashed and salted — we never store it in plain text
  • Firewalls: Multiple layers of security to prevent unauthorized access
  • Regular Audits: Periodic security reviews and vulnerability assessments

7.2 Organizational Measures

  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • All employees sign confidentiality agreements
  • Regular training on data protection best practices

7.3 Your Responsibility

While we do our best to secure your data, no system is 100% secure. You also play a role in keeping your account safe:

  • Use a strong, unique password
  • Never share your password with anyone
  • Log out from shared devices
  • Report suspicious activity immediately

8 Data Retention

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Active Accounts: Data is retained as long as your account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: Data is deleted within 30 days of account deletion (except where required by law)
  • Property Listings: Removed from public view immediately when marked sold/deleted
  • Legal Requirements: Some data may be retained longer if required by law (e.g., tax records, fraud investigations)
  • Backups: Backup copies may retain data for up to 90 days after deletion

9 Your Rights

Under India's DPDP Act, 2023, you have the following rights over your personal data:

Right to Access

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to Correct

Update or correct inaccurate personal information.

Right to Erase

Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").

Right to Object

Object to the processing of your data for certain purposes.

Right to Portability

Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent at any time for marketing communications.

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, email us at kaleomkar@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy Rights Request." We will respond within 30 days.

Right to Complain

If you're not satisfied with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with India's Data Protection Board of India under the DPDP Act, 2023.

10 Children's Privacy

EstateHaven is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at kaleomkar@gmail.com. We will delete such information promptly.

11 Third-Party Links

EstateHaven may contain links to third-party websites, plugins, or services (such as WhatsApp, social media, or maps). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties.

We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any third-party website you visit. This Privacy Policy applies only to information collected by EstateHaven.

12 International Users

EstateHaven is operated from India and is designed for users in India. If you access our Platform from outside India (e.g., NRIs), your information will be transferred to and processed in India.

By using EstateHaven, you consent to the transfer, storage, and processing of your information in India, where data protection laws may differ from those in your country.

13 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes:

  • We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page
  • We will notify registered users via email or in-app notification
  • Continued use of the Platform after changes means you accept the new policy

We recommend reviewing this policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your data.

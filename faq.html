The basics of EstateHaven and how it works.

EstateHaven is India's zero-brokerage real estate platform. We connect property owners directly with buyers and tenants — cutting out agents, commissions, and hidden fees.

Whether you're buying, renting, or selling a home, you deal directly with the person who owns the property. No middlemen, ever.

Three key differences:

  • Zero brokerage — We don't allow agents or brokers. Every listing is posted by a verified owner.
  • Direct contact — You reach the owner directly via WhatsApp, call, or email. No lead generation companies.
  • Transparent pricing — No inflated prices, no commission added to the property value.

Yes, absolutely free — for both property owners and buyers/renters. No listing fees, no subscription charges, no commissions, no hidden costs.

We never ask for money on behalf of any owner. If someone asks you to pay to "unlock" a listing on our platform, report them immediately.

We currently have listings across all major Indian cities: Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Lucknow, Aurangabad, and many smaller cities.

Everything about registration, login, and managing your profile.

Click the "Register as Seller" button in the top-right corner of any page. Complete the 4-step form:

Safety & Reporting

5 questions

Stay safe on EstateHaven and help us keep fraud out.

Follow these golden rules:

  • Always visit the property in person before any payment
  • Verify ownership documents (sale deed, tax receipts, encumbrance certificate)
  • Never share OTP, bank details, or pay advance without verification
  • Meet the owner at the property during daylight hours
  • Consult a lawyer before signing any agreement
  • Trust your instincts — if something feels off, walk away

If you spot a fake or suspicious listing:

  • Take a screenshot of the listing
  • Send details to kaleomkar@gmail.com with subject "Report Listing"
  • Or use our Contact form and select "Report a Problem"

We investigate all reports within 24 hours and take action against confirmed fraudsters.

EstateHaven has a strict zero-broker policy. If you suspect someone is an agent or broker, report them immediately with proof (screenshots, chat logs).

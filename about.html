Our Mission & Vision

We exist to remove every unnecessary barrier between property owners and the people who want to live in them.

Our Mission

To empower every Indian property owner to list, sell, or rent their property directly — with zero brokerage, full transparency, and instant reach to millions of genuine buyers and tenants.

Our Vision

To become India's most trusted real estate platform where every transaction happens between real people — no middlemen, no commissions, no hidden costs. Just honest deals powered by technology.

Our Story

EstateHaven was born out of frustration. Our founders spent months searching for a home and were met with the same story everywhere — pushy brokers, inflated prices, hidden charges, and endless commissions.

We realized the entire system was built to serve middlemen, not the people who actually owned or wanted to buy properties. So we decided to change it.

In 2024, we launched EstateHaven — a platform that puts the owner and buyer in direct contact. No brokerage. No commission. No inflated pricing. Just honest listings from verified owners across India.

Today, we're proud to be India's fastest-growing zero-brokerage real estate platform, serving families in Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad, Chennai, and beyond.

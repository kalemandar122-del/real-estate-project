Getting Started

Everything you need to know about EstateHaven — from signing up to posting your first listing.

EstateHaven is India's zero-brokerage real estate platform. We connect property owners directly with buyers and tenants — cutting out middlemen, commissions, and hidden fees.

Owners post properties for free. Buyers browse, filter, and contact owners directly via WhatsApp, call, or email. No agents involved, ever.

Yes, 100% free. There are no listing fees, no commission, and no hidden charges — for both owners and buyers.

You can post unlimited properties, upload photos, and receive direct inquiries without paying a single rupee.

EstateHaven currently serves all major Indian cities including Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Jaipur, Lucknow, and Aurangabad — plus many smaller cities across India.

If your city isn't listed, you can still post your property by selecting "Other" in the city dropdown.

Account & Security

Manage your account, login credentials, and keep your data safe.

Click "Register as Seller" in the top-right corner of any page. Fill in your personal details, contact info, address, and ID proof — it takes about 2 minutes.

Once registered, you'll be automatically logged in and can post your first property right away.

Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page. Enter your registered email or mobile number, and we'll send you a reset link.

If you don't receive the email within 10 minutes, check your spam folder or contact our support team at kaleomkar@gmail.com.

Your personal information is encrypted and stored securely. We never sell your data to third parties.

Your ID proof is used only for verification and is never shown publicly. Only your name, WhatsApp number, and email are displayed on your listings — and only to genuine buyers.

Log in to your account, click on your avatar in the top-right corner, and select "My Dashboard". From there, you can edit your profile, update your contact details, and change your preferences.

Listing a Property

Learn how to post, edit, and manage your property listings effectively.

Log in and click "Post Property" in the navigation menu. Fill in the 4-step form:

  • Basic Details — listing type, property type, BHK, furnishing
  • Location & Area — city, locality, address, built-up area
  • Photos & Pricing — upload photos, set your price
  • Review & Post — verify everything and submit

Your listing goes live instantly.

You can upload up to 15 photos per property. Accepted formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP. Maximum 5MB per photo.

We recommend uploading at least 5 high-quality photos — properties with more photos get up to 3x more views.

Go to "My Dashboard" from your avatar menu. You'll see all your properties listed there.

Click Edit to update details, or Delete to remove a listing. You can also mark a property as Sold or Rented once the deal is done.

If your listing isn't appearing, check these:

  • Did you complete all mandatory fields?
  • Is your property marked as "Active" (not Sold/Rented)?
  • Are you browsing with an active filter (city, budget) that excludes your property?

Try clicking Reset Filters on the listings page. If it still doesn't show, contact support.

Buying & Renting

Find your dream property and connect with owners directly.

Visit the Listings page. Use the search bar and filters to narrow down by:

  • City and locality
  • Property type (Apartment, House, Villa, Plot)
  • BHK configuration
  • Budget range
  • Furnishing status
  • Availability

Open any property listing. You'll see three direct contact buttons:

  • WhatsApp — sends a pre-filled message to the owner
  • Call — initiates a direct phone call
  • Email — opens your email client with a draft

All inquiries go straight to the verified owner — no middlemen.

Yes. Every property owner on EstateHaven is ID-verified using Aadhaar, PAN, or another government-issued ID. We do not allow brokers or agents on our platform.

If you ever encounter a suspicious listing, report it immediately using the contact form. We take fraud very seriously.

You can view all your saved properties by clicking "Saved Properties" in your account dropdown.

Payments & Fees

Understand our zero-brokerage model and how money flows on EstateHaven.

Absolutely not. EstateHaven is completely free for both owners and buyers. There are no listing fees, no subscription charges, no commission, and no hidden costs.

We don't handle payments between owners and buyers. All financial transactions happen directly between you and the property owner/tenant.

Traditional brokers typically charge 1-2% of the property value as commission — that's often ₹50,000 to ₹2 lakh+ on an average property.

With EstateHaven, that entire amount stays in your pocket. On rental properties, you save the equivalent of one month's rent (usually paid as brokerage).

Genuine owners will always be happy to show you the property first. If someone asks for money upfront without a site visit, it's a red flag — report them to us immediately.

Safety & Reporting

Stay safe on EstateHaven and help us keep the platform fraud-free.

Follow these golden rules:

  • Always visit the property in person before any payment
  • Verify ownership documents (sale deed, tax receipts, encumbrance certificate)
  • Never share OTP, bank details, or pay advance without verification
  • Meet the owner at the property during daylight hours
  • Consult a lawyer before signing any agreement
  • Trust your instincts — if something feels off, walk away

If you spot a fake listing, an agent posing as an owner, or any suspicious activity:

  • Open the listing and take a screenshot
  • Send details to kaleomkar@gmail.com with the subject "Report Listing"
  • Or use our Contact form and select "Report a Problem"

We investigate all reports within 24 hours and take action against confirmed fraudsters.

EstateHaven has a strict zero-broker policy. If you suspect someone is an agent or broker:

  • Report them immediately via the contact form
  • Include proof (screenshots, chat logs)
  • We will investigate and permanently ban the account if confirmed

Your vigilance helps keep EstateHaven a truly broker-free platform.
